## Where it started

I started Foxbrain Creative during college, with a small team of friends, in Cochin and Munnar. We made websites and digital presence for local, national and a few international clients.

## Tourism and hospitality

Most of our clients were hotels, homestays, adventure-activity operators and parks in and around Munnar. For a homestay in the hills, the website *is* the front desk, so we took on everything:

- brand identities and visual systems
- websites, designed and hand-built in HTML, CSS, JavaScript and Bootstrap
- social media assets
- photography and content for their campaigns

## What it taught me

Running a studio meant owning every project end to end: client discovery, concept, design, development, content and delivery. Every product role I've had since rests on that habit of seeing the whole thing through.

In parallel, I spent the last year at Teezonic Digital Makers as a UI designer, working on wireframes, prototypes, style guides and responsive interfaces.
