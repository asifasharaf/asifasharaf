## The studio

Gloworks Studio makes video and podcast content for brands in Bengaluru, from concept to final cut. It also rents out its space and gear.

## Two products in one codebase

- **The marketing site**: services, portfolio, studio space, gear rental, a free podcast guide and a blog.
- **/ops**: the studio's own back office. It is a lightweight CRM with leads, a deal pipeline, projects, bookings and rentals.

## Built for the device that's actually in hand

The rule for the ops side was mobile-first, for real. The target devices are a small tablet and a 13-inch laptop, with phones supported too. Every screen has to work at 768px before it counts as done. Tap targets are at least 40px, nothing depends on hover, and the site installs as a PWA.

The UI runs on a shadcn/ui system themed to the Gloworks brand. It is owned as source, not imported as a dependency.
