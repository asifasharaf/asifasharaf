I've wanted to build a proper website for a while. Not a portfolio in the LinkedIn sense — a place on the internet that feels like mine.

Something I keep coming back to: the web is the one medium where a designer can fully control the reading experience. No algorithm deciding what gets seen, no feed compressing your work into a thumbnail. Just you and whoever finds their way here.

That's worth something.

## What this is

This is where I'll write about design, process, and whatever else I'm thinking about. It's also where my work lives in its full form.

## What it isn't

It's not a content strategy. I'm not trying to build an audience. I'm just trying to have something of my own.
