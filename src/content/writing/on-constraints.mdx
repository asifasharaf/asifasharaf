The best briefs I've worked on had real constraints. Budget ceilings, tight timelines, weird technical requirements that seem arbitrary until you understand the business context.

For a long time I saw these as things to negotiate around. Now I see them as the brief itself.

Constraints don't limit the work. They define what the work is trying to do.
