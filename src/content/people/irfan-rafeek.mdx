## How we know each other

Irfan and I were product designers at Quintype at the same time, working on the publishing platform used by newsrooms like Fortune India and National Herald. He co-led the Bold CMS redesign, designed the Ahead page builder and helped build Quintype's design system. We also both worked on rethinking subscription flows for publishers.

Today we're building [Nimo](/work/nimo) together, a clinical note system for Indian clinics. His years in publishing are where Nimo's core idea came from: treat a clinical note like a document made of blocks, not a fixed medical form.

## His work

- **Condé Nast, 2020 – 2025.** Principal Product Designer on Verso, the design system behind more than 20 brands including Vogue, The New Yorker, Wired and Architectural Digest. He reworked its token and theming architecture so sub-brands and components could be themed, and his spacing tokens were adopted by 22 brands.
- **Ambo, 2025 – now.** Founding designer at an employee-advocacy startup, taking it from concept to a multi-workflow content platform in six months.
- **Impostor Games, 2026.** A browser party game he designed and built with AI-assisted design-to-code. It reached players in more than 40 countries in its first month.
- **Before that:** Quintype, YourStory, and front-end work at Red Panthers.
